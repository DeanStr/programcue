1. Who operates Program Cue

Program Cue is based in Victoria, Australia ("Program Cue", "we", "us"). Program Cue is responsible for the personal information described in this policy and is the contact for privacy enquiries and requests.

Contact: support@programcue.com

2. What this policy covers

Program Cue is a conference programme operations platform. Conference organisers use it to run submissions, reviews, speaker readiness, communications and programme scheduling for their events.

Two relationships matter for privacy, and this policy covers both:

  • Information we hold as the operator. Your account, your sign-in details, security logs and anything you send us directly. We decide how this information is handled.
  • Information we host on behalf of an organiser. Submissions, reviews, speaker records, schedules and communications belong to the organisation running the event. We process that information so the organisers can run their event, under their instructions. If your information is in an event because an organiser put it there, that organisation decides what happens to it, and its own privacy notice applies alongside this one. We will help you reach the right organiser if you are not sure who that is.

This website, programcue.com, is a static set of pages. It sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name and email address.
  • Profile information held against your record: your display name, a biography, how your name is pronounced, your job title, your organisation and a profile image — whether you enter it yourself or an organiser enters it for you.
  • Authentication identifiers: the account identifier your sign-in provider gives us when you use Google or Microsoft sign-in, the single-use email sign-in links we issue, and your session records. Program Cue does not use or store passwords.

Organisation, event and programme data

  • The organisations and events you belong to, and the roles and permissions assigned to you within them.
  • Submissions and their content, including proposal text, speaker details, answers to the questions an organiser configures, and revision history.
  • Reviews, scores, review comments, reviewer assignments, conflicts of interest you declare, round outcomes and decisions.
  • Speaker records: confirmations, readiness status, requirements, session materials and outstanding tasks.
  • Scheduling data: sessions, rooms, tracks, times, time zones and published programme entries.
  • Communications data: message templates, recipients, message content, send and delivery status, bounce and complaint reports returned by our email provider, and unsubscribe state.

Uploaded files

  • Files you or an organiser upload — for example slides, papers, headshots and supporting documents — together with their filename, size, content type and upload metadata.
  • Uploaded files are scanned for malware before they are made available. A file that fails scanning is quarantined and not served.

Technical and security logs

  • Request logs generated when you use the application, including IP address, user agent, request path, timestamp, response status and a request correlation identifier.
  • Security and audit records: sign-in attempts and outcomes, access and permission changes, consequential actions taken in an event, and abuse-prevention signals. Sign-in is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which assesses whether a request is automated.
  • Error reports and diagnostic traces used to keep the service working.

Cookies and session data

  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary — without it the application cannot tell who you are.
  • Short-lived cookies used during a sign-in or calendar connection to protect the exchange against interference. These expire within minutes.
  • Cookies set by Cloudflare Turnstile as part of its automated-abuse checks.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • Provide the features of the platform: submissions, reviewing, speaker readiness, scheduling, communications and calendar invitations.
  • Give organisers the operational view they need to run their event, within the roles and permissions they have assigned.
  • Send transactional and programme email you would expect from the event you are part of, such as sign-in links, submission confirmations, decision notices, reminders and speaker updates.
  • Keep the service secure and available: prevent abuse, investigate incidents, diagnose faults, maintain backups and meet our record-keeping obligations.
  • Respond to you when you contact us.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information, or any data obtained through an integration, for advertising or for training generative AI models.

Program Cue includes optional assistant features that an organisation can enable. When they are used, the relevant programme content is sent to the configured AI provider to produce a suggestion. The default provider is Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs on the same Cloudflare infrastructure as the rest of the platform. Assistant suggestions are proposals: a person reviews and confirms them before they take effect.

5. Google user data

This section describes exactly what Program Cue accesses from Google, why, and what happens to it. It applies in addition to the rest of this policy.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, Program Cue requests the openid, email and profile scopes. We use them only to authenticate you and to create or match your Program Cue account. From this we store your Google account identifier, your email address, your name and, where Google provides one, a reference to your profile image.

Optional Google Calendar connection

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Program Cue works without it. If you choose to connect it, Program Cue requests one scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned

Program Cue uses that Calendar permission only to create, update, cancel and reconcile Program Cue-managed session invitations on the connected user's primary calendar. Reconciliation means checking that the invitations Program Cue created still match the scheduled session, and correcting them when a session is moved, changed or cancelled.

The information involved in those invitations may include the event title, description, location, time zone, start and end times, attendee details and invitation response status.

Program Cue does not provide general calendar browsing. It does not read, index or display your other calendar entries, and it does not use Calendar data for advertising.

Storage, retention and revocation

  • OAuth tokens for the calendar connection are encrypted at rest and retained while the calendar connection is active.
  • Disconnecting removes the stored calendar credentials once any active invitation obligations have been resolved — for example, once invitations Program Cue is responsible for have been updated or cancelled. Some security, audit or transaction records may remain under the general retention policy in section 10.
  • You can also revoke Program Cue's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access there stops further calendar operations; any invitations already issued remain in the attendees' calendars until they are changed or removed.

Sharing and limited use

Program Cue does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not transfer it to others except as necessary to provide the feature you asked for, to comply with applicable law, or as part of the infrastructure providers listed in section 7 acting on our instructions.

The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Microsoft and other integrations

Microsoft sign-in works the same way as Google Sign-In: Program Cue requests openid, email and profile, and uses them only to authenticate you and to create or match your account.

A Microsoft calendar connection is also optional and is used only to create, update, cancel and reconcile Program Cue-managed session invitations. Its tokens are encrypted at rest and removed on disconnection on the same terms as the Google connection.

An organisation may also choose to connect Program Cue to Accelevents or Airtable. Those credentialed connections are configured by the organisation and move event and programme data between Program Cue and the selected service.

Separately, a verified applicant may ask Program Cue to read one public Sessionize speaker profile. Program Cue imports only the public name and biography into the applicant's unsaved draft for review. It does not sign in to Sessionize, request Sessionize credentials, access private Sessionize data or create an ongoing organisation connection.

The receiving service's own terms and privacy notice apply to data it holds.

7. Service providers and infrastructure

Program Cue runs on a small number of providers. Each processes personal information on our instructions, for the purposes below and no other:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and application runtime, the database, uploaded-file storage, backups, DNS and content delivery, Turnstile abuse prevention, malware scanning of uploads, logging and the default AI assistant provider.
  • Google — Google Sign-In, and the optional Google Calendar connection described in section 5.
  • Microsoft — Microsoft sign-in, and the optional Microsoft calendar connection described in section 6.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional and programme email, including delivery, bounce and complaint reporting.

This list reflects the current production configuration. If we add or change a provider that handles personal information, we will update this policy as described in section 13.

We may also disclose information where we are required to by law, or where it is necessary to investigate a security incident or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

8. Where information is processed

Program Cue is operated from Australia, but the providers in section 7 operate globally. Your information may be processed and stored outside Australia, including in the United States and in Europe. Our malware scanning of uploaded files is constrained to European regions.

Where information is transferred overseas, we rely on the provider's contractual data-protection commitments and take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with this policy and with Australian privacy law.

9. Storage and security

Information is stored in Cloudflare's database and object storage. Uploaded files are private by default and are served only through authorised, time-limited access. Traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Integration credentials and calendar OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest.

Access is enforced on the server for every request, based on the organisation, event and role you have been assigned; hiding a control in the interface is never treated as authorisation on its own. A production export has been restored to an isolated database as a recovery exercise. Scheduled production backups, rolling retention, backup alerting and a measured incident restore have not yet been completed.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

10. How long information is kept

  • Account information is kept while your account exists. After deletion, some account data may remain in security, audit, transaction or recovery-validation records described below, or where applicable law requires it.
  • Event and programme data is kept for as long as the organisation running the event needs it. Program Cue provides organisers with retention tools so they can review and remove participant data for a completed event.
  • Uploaded files are kept while the submission or session they belong to is retained, or until they are deleted by you or an organiser.
  • Calendar OAuth tokens are kept while the connection is active, then removed as described in section 5.
  • Security, audit and transaction records are kept for as long as we need them to investigate incidents, prevent abuse and meet our legal obligations. These may outlive the deletion of other records.
  • Backup exports created for recovery validation may retain copies after live records are deleted. These exports do not currently follow a fixed rolling production retention schedule.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal information we hold about you.
  • Give you a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete information we no longer need to hold.
  • Close your account.

You can also disconnect a calendar connection at any time from your Program Cue settings, revoke access from your Google or Microsoft account, and unsubscribe from non-essential programme email using the link in those messages. Messages that are necessary to operate your account, such as sign-in links, cannot be unsubscribed from while the account is open.

Email support@programcue.com to make a request. We will verify who you are before acting, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

Where your information sits inside an organisation's event because an organiser put it there, we may need to refer your request to that organisation, or act on its instructions, because it decides what happens to that data. We will tell you when that is the case and help you reach them.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy matter, contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

12. Children

Program Cue is a tool for people running and taking part in professional conference programmes. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and its production configuration change. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page and publish the updated policy here.

If a change materially affects how we handle your personal information — for example, a new purpose or a new category of recipient — we will give you notice by email or in the application before it takes effect, so you have a reasonable opportunity to review it.
