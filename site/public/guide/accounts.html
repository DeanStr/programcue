Accounts and access

An account is only your login. You still need an invitation or a published application link before you can see an event.

How you get into an event

You cannot create an organisation by signing up. Workspaces are created for a conference team, then people are invited in.

  • An organiser invited you. Open the invitation, sign in and accept it.
  • You have a published application link. Open it and start the form. That can give you submitter access.
  • You were named as a co-speaker. Use the claim link in that email. Your workspace access starts when you claim, not when your name was typed on the form.
  • You run the conference and do not have a workspace yet. Write to support@programcue.com.

Once you are an owner or administrator of an organisation, you can create or clone further events from New event. See Set up an event.

Sign in

  1. Open app.programcue.com/sign-in.
  2. Request an email sign-in link, or continue with Google or Microsoft.
  3. If you signed in with Microsoft and Program Cue asks you to confirm your email, finish that step before the accounts are linked.

After you sign in, Program Cue opens the screen for your role: Event settings for owners and administrators, Review & selection for committee chairs, the Review workbench for evaluators, and the participant workspace for speakers and submitters.

If you see “no workspace access”

That page means your login exists, but nobody has given this account an event yet. That is expected after a new sign-up.

Use the invitation or application link you were sent. If you think you should already have access, ask the event organiser or write to support@programcue.com.

Roles

Each person has a role on a specific event. Removing a control from the screen is not what grants access; Program Cue checks the role when you try to do the work.

  • Owner and administrator run the event: settings, applications, speakers, schedule, messages and publication. Organisation-wide owners and administrators can also create further events.
  • Committee chair runs Review & selection for that event.
  • Evaluator scores only assigned work in the Review workbench. See Review assigned proposals.
  • Submitter and speaker use the participant workspace. See Speak or submit.

If you belong to more than one event

Choose the event before you continue. Program Cue remembers the one you picked. Pending invitations stay in that list until you accept them.

What people can see without signing in

Attendees do not need an account to read a published programme, timetable, speaker gallery, itinerary, calendar feed or event website. Those pages only show the published version. Planning, reviews, files and drafts stay inside the event workspace.
