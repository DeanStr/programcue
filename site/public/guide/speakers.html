Speakers, tasks and resources

After you accept a proposal, use Speakers, Tasks and Speaker resources to keep people ready. Speakers do their own work in the participant workspace.

What speakers see

Speakers and submitters work in the participant workspace. Point them to Speak or submit. This page is the organiser side of the same work.

Onboard accepted speakers

When you accept a proposal, Program Cue can create the session, the speaker records and the starting task list in one step. Speakers then finish profile, file and acknowledgement work themselves.

If you add a speaker yourself, their membership stays pending until they accept the invitation, or until you record that you confirmed them outside Program Cue.

Tasks

  1. Open Tasks to edit templates and live tasks. A task can be an acknowledgement, a short form, a file upload, a link visit, a checklist or an organiser-only item.
  2. Set due dates from acceptance or from session time. Later tasks can wait on earlier ones.
  3. Preview bulk changes, confirm them, and undo a completion when that undo is still safe.

Overdue tasks show in Command Centre and in the administration notifications.

Speaker resources

Open Speaker resources to write pages for participants: categories, attachments, allowed embeds and who should see each page. Publish a page, then attach an acknowledgement task if you need proof they read it.

These pages belong to your event. They are not this product guide.

Files

Speakers upload headshots, slides and supporting documents. Those files stay private and are held until the required file check finishes. A failed or unclear check does not release the file.

Session content & files is your view of the same library, including versions. Data retention holds the keep-or-delete rules and any legal hold.

Speaker network

Open Speaker network to find people who are not yet on this event: contacts, a sourcing pipeline and invitation outreach. Merging two contacts asks you to confirm and shows which records will be combined.
