Review and selection

Committee chairs and organisers run Review & selection. Evaluators score only their assignments in the Review workbench.

Plan rounds and rubrics

Open Review & selection. Each round has its own dates, blind- review setting, evaluator pool and scorecard.

  • Changing a draft scorecard creates a new version. You can reuse a scorecard only when it still matches exactly.
  • Criteria can mix numbers and comments. A dropdown must have an ordered list of options.
  • A round can review applications, sessions, or both.

Invite evaluators

Invite people as evaluators from Review & selection. After they accept, they work in the Review workbench. Point them to Review assigned proposals.

Removing an evaluator asks you to confirm and cancels their unfinished assignments. Those assignments come back only if you add the same person to the same pool again.

Assign work

Create assignments in Review & selection. Evaluators see only what you assigned. In a blind round they see only answers marked as review content.

What evaluators do

They score in the Review workbench, save as they go, submit when finished and recuse if they have a conflict. AI suggestions, when you have turned them on, stay labelled as suggestions until the evaluator accepts them.

Moderate and reopen

Chairs can moderate a finished review, reopen one that needs another look, and read discussion on that proposal or session in the round. Reopening asks for a new score. It does not rewrite the old one.

Release decisions

  1. Preview the decisions before you release them.
  2. Accepting a proposal can create the session, the speaker records and the onboarding tasks in one step. Confirm that change.
  3. Release stops if the decision email template, verified sender or recipient is not ready. Nothing is recorded until those are in place. If a send fails after a successful release, that failure stays visible on the send.
