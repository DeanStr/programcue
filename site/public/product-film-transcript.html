Product film transcript

A text equivalent of the six-minute, music-led film, including its visual narrative and on-screen product evidence.

0:00 — The programme journey

[Instrumental music.] Scene 1 — Program Cue opens the programme journey. Three current product views frame the programme’s starting point. The message is “From first proposal to published programme.”

0:15 — One connected workspace

Scene 2 — One workspace maps the programme lifecycle. Shape, Decide and Share organise the connected steps. The Command Centre replaces the lifecycle diagram.

0:31 — Event readiness

Scene 3 — The Command Centre shows event readiness. Readiness is 75 percent across six workflows. Two blockers appear in the action queue. A filtered task list exposes owner and impact.

0:54 — Event settings

Scene 4 — Event settings define shared identity and access. Event data authority is Program Cue or Airtable. Branding previews appear at desktop and mobile sizes. A confirmed publish step makes the identity public.

1:17 — Applications

Scene 5 — Five steps govern a versioned application. Questions include conditional logic and a review purpose. Desktop and mobile previews remain clearly labelled. Publishing opens stable form version 01 for applications. A verified applicant saves a private draft. Submission keeps answers with form version 01.

1:50 — Reviews and decisions

Scene 6 — An evaluator opens the review queue. The workbench combines answers, version and conflict status. Four weighted scores produce a total of 4.25. Advisory suggestions cite evidence for optional import. The reviewer submits a waitlist recommendation. Two reviews support a released waitlist decision.

2:26 — Speaker preparation

Scene 7 — An accepted session opens speaker preparation. Desktop and mobile workspaces show the linked session. One onboarding task is complete; two remain open. The speaker deck completes secure file checks. Event imagery connects speaker preparation to show day.

2:59 — Event Assistant

Scene 8 — The Command Centre opens the Event Assistant. The organiser asks for a draft reminder for approval. Priya has one incomplete speaker-scoped task. The reminder template exposes supported merge fields. One speaker-scoped recipient awaits approval. The reminder enters the queue. Operation Centre shows approval and queue status.

3:34 — Communications

Scene 9 — A speaker welcome opens for review. Alex Morgan’s personalised preview appears. Confirmation rechecks the recipient and template. The delivery status records the welcome handoff result. Calendar work keeps a separate queue status.

4:00 — Schedule publication

Scene 10 — Conflict-aware scheduling moves towards publication. Two Main Hall sessions overlap at 10:30. Two placements are valid; a third session remains unplaced. Content and speaker checks remain separate. The publication diff preserves the current public programme. Final confirmation revalidates every dependency. One approved schedule version becomes public.

4:40 — Public programme

Scene 11 — Seven views share published programme data. Programme and timetable views appear on two screen sizes. A mobile programme filters to one selected date. Speaker profiles link people to published sessions. A personal itinerary holds four saved sessions. A managed embed shows the published programme. Light, dark and mobile site views appear. Event imagery brings the programme to life.

5:16 — Integrations and recovery

Program Cue connects to your stack through 33 documented API paths. The contract explorer shows scoped keys and schemas. Operation recovery follows one reminder to a failed result. The failed operation keeps its original one-person audience. An Accelevents preview maps one confirmation-ready export.

5:42 — One connected system

Scene 13 — The product journey resolves into one system. One programme. Every workflow. Fully connected. The final card shows ProgramCue.com with three product snapshots. Picture fades.
